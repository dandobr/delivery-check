Delivery photos vs. packing list

Upload the packing list (PDF) and up to 3 delivery photos. Every row gets a status with a cited photo region. There is no "missing" status: an item that isn't visible is unverified, never assumed absent.

Capture convention: every item carries a numbered position tag (POS-1, POS-2, …) next to its product label, visible in every photo it appears in. Objects are counted by tag, so the same box in two photos is counted once.
